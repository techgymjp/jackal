[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You play rugby in a stadium at night. A player from the other team runs with the ball. Stop him with a tackle. Then take the ball away from his team. In rugby, this is called a "jackal". There are 20 plays in one game.

■ PLAYER A: TACKLE THE RUNNER
• Sometimes the runner runs away from you. Run after him. When you are close, press the forward tackle button.
• Sometimes the runner runs toward you. Just before he reaches you, he steps to the left or to the right. Press the tackle button for that side.
• If you tackle too early, he runs past you. In later plays, he also tries to trick you.
• When the runner gets close to the try line, the crowd blows horns.

■ PLAYER B: GET THE LOOSE BALL
• After a good tackle, you control Player B. Player B was running next to the runner.
• A tackled player must let go of the ball. The ball rolls on the ground.
• Run to the ball. Touch it before the other team does. Then you win the ball.
• In later plays, the ball bounces in a new direction.

■ 3D VIEW FROM BEHIND YOUR PLAYER
• You see the stadium from behind your player.
• You can see the crowd, the goal posts, the referee and both teams.
• The game shows the tackle in slow motion.
• A small map shows the players and the ball.

■ CHEERS AND FIREWORKS
• The crowd plays drums and claps.
• When you win the ball, you hear a whistle and a big cheer, and you see fireworks.
• If you are fast, you get more points.
• After 5 wins in a row, you see more fireworks.

■ EASY CONTROLS
• Move with the round control on the left.
• Use the buttons on the right to tackle forward, left or right, and to run faster.
• Player B only moves and runs faster. He takes the ball when he touches it.
• Turn on NAV for help. NAV shows when your tackle will reach the runner. It also shows where the ball will roll.

■ MORE
• You get a rank at the end of the game.
• You can share your score with friends.
• If you lose a play, you can watch an ad and play it again. You can do this up to 5 times in one game.
• You can play without the internet. Ads and sharing need the internet.
• The game is in English and Japanese.
