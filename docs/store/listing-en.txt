[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
The floodlights are on, the stands are packed, and their ball carrier is coming.
Put him down, then rip the ball away. Live the rugby "jackal" across 20 plays.

■ PLAYER A: STOP THE RUNNER
• He breaks away? CHASE HIM! Sprint after him and dive at his ankles with a forward tackle
• He comes straight at you? STOP HIM! He sidesteps right in front of you, so tackle left or right to the side he goes
• Dive in too early and he steps around you. Later plays bring feints
• The crowd's cheer horns blare as he closes in on the try line

■ PLAYER B: WIN THE LOOSE BALL
• Make the tackle and you switch to Player B, who was running alongside
• The tackled player must release the ball at once, and it comes bouncing loose
• Get to it before their support player and it's a jackal. Turnover!
• Later on, the oval ball takes wicked bounces and changes direction

■ 3D VIEW FROM BEHIND YOUR PLAYER
• Packed stands, goal posts, a referee and both teams all around you
• Slow motion at the moment of the tackle
• A mini-map shows where the players and the ball are

■ FIREWORKS AND CHEERS FOR EVERY JACKAL
• Drums and clapping from the stands, and a rising roar as the tackle gets close
• A whistle, a huge cheer and fireworks every time you win the ball
• Faster reactions score more. Extra fireworks every 5 jackals in a row

■ EASY TO PICK UP
• Move with the direction ring, and use the buttons to tackle forward, left or right and to dash
• Player B just runs and dashes. Touch the ball and he grabs it
• Turn on NAV to see when your tackle will connect and where the ball will roll

■ FEATURES
• Ranks from Weekend Hacker to Legendary Jackal
• Share your score and rank with friends (shows whether NAV was on)
• Lost a play? Watch an ad to try it again (up to 5 times per game)
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

Kick off! Can you win the ball on all 20 plays?
